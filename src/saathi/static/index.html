      Directions are the part of a delivery that goes wrong when two people do not share a language. A rider who misses one number or one landmark loses minutes at the wrong gate.

      What to know

      • Ten languages, any to any: Bengali, English, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu.
      • Kannada, English, Hindi and Bengali are evaluated on a synthetic test set; the other six are beta (machine-verified only) and marked as such on screen. The author reviewed all Bengali and Hindi interface text, quick replies, repeat prompts and the clip prompt.
      • The facts on the tiles are read from the speech by rules, then shown for the rider to confirm or question. Check anything marked “check this”.
      • Rooms live in memory and expire. Transcripts are not stored.
