# Kannada recording script (for the Saathi evaluation)

Thank you for helping. Saathi is a student portfolio project: a customer gives directions by voice
and a delivery rider who speaks another language hears them translated. We test whether the speech
recognition gets the address facts (gate, floor, cross, main, landmark) right.

## What you agree to
- You record about 10 short clips of someone giving directions to a delivery rider. All addresses
  below are **invented**; none is a real address. Do not say your own address or anyone's phone number.
- The clips are used only to test the project. They stay on Rahul's laptop, are **not** put on
  GitHub or shared, and are described in the write-up only as "consented human speech".
- Clips are sent to the Gnani speech service (a company that converts speech to text) for the
  test. Your name is not attached.
- You can change your mind and ask for the clips to be deleted at any time.
- Replying "yes, I agree" to Rahul's message is your consent.

## How to record
1. Quiet room, phone voice recorder app, phone about 20 cm from your mouth.
2. Press record, wait one second without speaking, say the sentence, wait one second, stop.
3. Speak the way you would to a delivery person on the phone: natural speed, natural words.
   **You may change the words** to how you really say it. Mixing Kannada and English is exactly
   what we want. If you change something, write what you actually said in the last column.
4. One clip per sentence. If you stumble, record that sentence again and keep the better one.
5. Name the files `kn01`, `kn02`, ... (the app's default extension, for example `.m4a`, is fine;
   send the original files, do not convert them).
6. Send the files and the filled-in table to Rahul by Oct 15.
